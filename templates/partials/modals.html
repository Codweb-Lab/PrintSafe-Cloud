PrintSafe Cloud — Complete Help Center

In-depth guide to Zero-Trust Vault, Multi-Cloud Mounting, QR Desktop Guard & Vector Printing
1. Connecting & Mounting Google Drive / OneDrive
  1. Open Google Drive or OneDrive in the left sidebar (or Profile Menu → Cloud Drive Manager) and click Connect Account / Add Account.
  2. Assign an optional custom display label (e.g., "Work Drive" or "College Docs"). If left blank, your email address is used automatically. You can rename any account anytime inside Cloud Drive Manager.
  3. Click the Folder () icon beside an active drive (or Browse in Cloud Manager) to open the right-side Cloud Drive Explorer.
  4. Click any folder name to drill down inside, and click Mount on the folders/files you want in your Vault. Only mounted items appear in your workspace.
  5. Use the toggle switch in Cloud Manager to temporarily hide/show an account without losing its mounted folders.
2. Uploading Files & Hosting SFTP / FTP Storage
  • Zero-Local-Storage Uploads: PrintSafe Cloud never stores your files on the web server. Open any mounted Google Drive, OneDrive, or SFTP/FTP folder and click the green Upload to [Folder] button at the bottom right to upload directly into that cloud/remote folder.
  • cPanel / VPS Storage: Click Hosting SFTP/FTP in the left sidebar to mount your server directory via SFTP (Port 22) or FTP (Port 21).
  • Right-Click Context Menu: Right-click any folder to Unmount it from your Vault (your original cloud files remain untouched), or right-click a file to permanently delete it from the source storage.
3. Zero-Trace Vector Printing & "To Be Printed" Queue
  • In-Memory Document Preview: Click any PDF, Image (JPG/PNG/WEBP), or Jupyter Notebook (.ipynb) to stream and render it directly in your browser's RAM without saving anything to the computer's Downloads folder.
  • Password-Protected PDFs: Encrypted PDFs show a Locked badge; click them and enter the PDF password to unlock and print in memory.
  • Clean Vector Print: Click Print in the top-right preview bar to print crisp pages with zero browser headers/footers or temporary files.
  • To Be Printed Queue: Click the Printer () icon on any file card to bookmark documents you plan to print at a cyber café or print shop.
4. QR Desktop Guard (Safe Printing on Public PCs)
  1. Never type your password on a shared/shop computer. Open PrintSafe Cloud on the shop PC—a live QR Code appears on the login screen.
  2. On your phone, sign in to PrintSafe Cloud and tap the blue QR Scan () button at the bottom right.
  3. Scan the desktop QR code. The desktop logs in under Restricted QR Mode (uploads, deletions, and cloud settings are locked).
  4. From your phone's Connected Devices () panel, you can live-toggle Mask Folder Names ("xxxxx"), disable profile access, or click Logout Device to remotely terminate the desktop session the moment your print finishes.
5. Account Security: Password, Quick PIN & Authenticator 2FA
  • Change Password: Open Profile Menu → Password, PIN & 2FA to update your login password anytime.
  • Quick Security PIN: Set a 4 or 6-digit PIN for fast sign-in on trusted personal devices.
  • 2FA Authenticator App: Link Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy to require a rotating 6-digit TOTP code on every login.
  • UI Themes & Design Systems: Switch between Glass Bento, Microsoft Fluent, IBM Carbon, and Cyber Neon in Light, Dark, or System mode from the top header.
